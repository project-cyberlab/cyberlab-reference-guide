Usage: /usr/bin/migrate-pubring-from-classic-gpg [GPGHOMEDIR|--default]
	Migrate public keyring in GPGHOMEDIR from "classic" to "modern" GnuPG
	using gpg version 2.2.

	--default migrates the GnuPG home directory at "/root/.gnupg"
